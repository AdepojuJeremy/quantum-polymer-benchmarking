# Dataset provenance

**Dataset:** PolyMetriX Curated Tg

**Target:** Tg (K)

**Usable polymers after the notebook's validation pipeline:** 7,363

**Upstream source:** https://zenodo.org/records/15210035/files/LAMALAB_CURATED_Tg_structured_polymerclass.csv?download=1

The raw upstream dataset is intentionally not copied into this release package.
The notebook downloads it from the recorded source, validates its schema,
records a SHA-256 checksum, and constructs the cleaned research dataframe.
